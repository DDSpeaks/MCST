MCST-Watchdog 1.20.13 - source validation, 27 September 2026
================================================================

Scope
-----
- Added an exact-fingerprint MC17 production AutoTrading profile for
  Charting.dll timestamp 0x6AB57EE6 and image size 18624512.
- Uses the dynamically verified MC17 strategy vtable RVA 0xB74CF0 and
  AutoTrading field offset 0x18.

  MultiCharts.exe and to exclude the current Watchdog process explicitly.

Research evidence
-----------------
- Five snapshots and four controlled transitions were captured on MC17.
- The selected MC17 field responded exactly in all 4/4 transitions.
- Both directions were verified: two 1->0 and two 0->1 changes.
- The selected field had zero unchanged or ambiguous transitions.
- No other candidate achieved a 4/4 full reverse-verified match.

Safety
------
- Both production profiles require an exact PE timestamp and image-size match.
- Unknown Charting.dll builds remain rejected and AutoTrading stays UNKNOWN.
- Monitoring remains passive and read-only; it does not click MultiCharts,
  send input, place orders, change AutoTrading, or write to MultiCharts memory.

Validation status

- Windows Release x64 compilation and live MC17 production-count confirmation
