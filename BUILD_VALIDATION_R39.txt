Required functional checks
--------------------------
1. Confirm OVERALL STATUS is bold, separated from the component rows, has a
   largest dot in the exact same vertical column as every component dot,
   and does not repeat its state.
   Inspect the HTML if necessary: `font-size:2em` belongs to the nested
   `mcst-status-overall-dot`, never the outer fixed-width `mcst-status-dot-cell`.
   Every component dot must use the nested `mcst-status-dot` at `1.5em`, so its
   color remains immediately recognizable on a mobile screen.
2. Confirm System Status descriptions and all Latest Activity values begin in
   identical columns on desktop and iOS Mail.
3. Confirm positive and negative Accounts numbers end at the same character
   column, including values with a leading minus sign.
4. Confirm every Open Positions record stays on one logical line and all Open
   P/L amounts and totals share the same vertical column.
5. With no open positions, confirm the section says `(no rows)` and
   omits the nine position column headings while retaining monthly results.
6. With two visible Accounts, confirm each has a separate current-month
   Realized P/L and a Position History row for a third account is absent.
   Confirm Total Open P/L and current-month Realized P/L rows have an empty
   third/comment cell: no bracketed row counts or currency explanations may
   widen the report.
7. Verify DMY, MDY, and YMD samples or set [Tracker] date_order explicitly.
8. Leave a verified V147 Tracker read stale past a recovery cooldown and confirm
   `tabview_recovery_start`, `tabview_recovery_scan`, and a final recovery result
   are written to the execution trace.
9. Confirm persistent failure retries remain under the Watchdog deadline and
   expanded/wide attempts occur no more often than their documented cooldowns.
10. Confirm the Dashboard or Status Report includes the CATPTTabView decision,
    candidate count, scores, and scan counters.
11. Open the MultiCharts Saxo authentication browser. Confirm an authentication
   alert after the configured delay and privacy-safe diagnostics.
12. Confirm `BrokerAuthDetector.cpp` compiles with the installed Windows SDK;
   COM/OLE headers must precede `UIAutomation.h` and this translation unit must
   not define `WIN32_LEAN_AND_MEAN`.
13. Enable Developer Mode, press Help, and confirm the left pane lists Overview,
    AT Start, AT Capture, AT Finish, Tracker Capture, Position CCY, Open Compat,
    and Reload Compat. Select every topic and confirm its complete instructions
    appear in the right pane.
14. In AT Capture, confirm the preparation explicitly says to choose one
    strategy on one chart and change only its AutoTrading state ON-to-OFF or
    OFF-to-ON. Confirm the page also identifies the output, next step, failure
    recovery, and read-only safety boundary.
15. Confirm Developer mode is unchecked on a new/default configuration, is
    located to the right of Reload Settings, immediately shows/hides the
    research toolbar, and persists `[Developer] enabled` after restart.
16. Confirm every research button is 20 pixels high and visibly shorter than
    the 34-pixel normal production buttons.
17. Confirm the Help window visibly says Developer tools are normally used only
    after a MultiCharts update or developer request. Open every topic and
    confirm its text also covers changed Charting.dll/ATOnPTracker.dll
    fingerprints and says the tools are not for normal monitoring.
18. Start two MultiCharts instances, one empty and one with AutoTrading. Confirm
    MultiCharts Health is the first row and reports two responsive instances.
19. Create a visible processing backlog and confirm the `q / s` values reach the
    Dashboard/report. Confirm a transient below 3 seconds stays OK, 3 seconds is
    WARNING, and 10 seconds or 1,000 queued items is CRITICAL.
20. Close the empty instance and confirm a temporary WARNING while AutoTrading
    remains above its minimum. Repeat with the trading instance and confirm the
    forced AutoTrading read makes the missing-strategy condition CRITICAL.
21. Use a controlled test process/window to hold UI response for three refresh
    samples. Confirm CRITICAL and a recovery transition after response returns.
22. Sustain one MultiCharts process above 90% of one logical core for three
    refresh samples and confirm a WARNING without creating a tight polling loop.
