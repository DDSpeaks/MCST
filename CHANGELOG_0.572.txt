MCST-Watchdog 0.572 - AutoTrading Candidate Verifier

- Adds a passive candidate verifier for the updated MultiCharts Charting.dll.
- Uses the known current AutoTrading ON count (default 18) to rank vtable RVA + boolean offset pairs.
- Aggregates only candidates shared by all detected MultiCharts processes.
- Requires at least 95% valid boolean values at a candidate offset.
- Writes C:\Temp\MCST-Watchdog-AutoTrading-Candidate-Verification-0.572.txt.
- Does not activate any candidate in the production AutoTrading reader.
- The known count can be changed in [AutoTradingDiagnostics] expected_active_strategies.
