MCST-Watchdog 0.575 - AutoTrading Dynamic Field Change Detector

- Replaces the fixed eight-candidate toggle verifier with a dynamic memory change detector.
- Captures all reasonable Charting.dll vtable candidates (2-512 objects) and 0x300 bytes per object.
- Compares the same PID, vtable RVA and object address after one strategy is switched ON -> OFF.
- Reports every stable boolean byte transition 1 -> 0 and unexpected 0 -> 1 transition.
- Highlights stable vtable populations and strategy-sized populations (15-25 objects).
- Remains fully passive: no clicks, input, or writes to MultiCharts memory.
- Production AutoTrading reader remains unchanged until a field is verified.
