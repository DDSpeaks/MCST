MCST-Watchdog 0.577 - Automated AutoTrading Research Session
============================================================

- Replaces manual candidate-by-candidate comparison with one guided research session.
- Adds Start AT Research, Capture AT Snapshot and Finish AT Research buttons.
- Writes every measurement to one persistent file:
  C:\Temp\MCST-Watchdog\AutoTradingResearch.txt
- Each capture appends a complete snapshot and automatic delta analysis.
- Finish Research ranks candidates by exact complementary ON/OFF responses.
- The user does not need to remember RVA values, offsets or compare candidates manually.
- Requires the same MultiCharts process set throughout the session.
- Passive diagnostics only; no clicks, input or writes to MultiCharts memory.
