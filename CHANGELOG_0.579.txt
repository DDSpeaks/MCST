MCST-Watchdog 0.579 - AutoTrading Settings, Alerts and Email
================================================================

- AutoTrading current count continues to come from the verified MC memory reader.
- minimum_active_strategies and check_interval_minutes are loaded from MCST-Watchdog.ini.
- Added Reload Settings without restarting the application.

- Dashboard Last Alert, Email status and Recent Activity are updated from real events.
