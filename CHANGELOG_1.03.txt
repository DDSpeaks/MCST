MCST-Watchdog 1.03 - Broker Recovery Detection Fix
===================================================

- Added semantic broker recovery detection for provider-specific messages.
- Recognizes messages such as "Connection with TradeStation established."
  without requiring an exact contiguous phrase match.
- Recovery events now take precedence over generic disconnect wording.
- Added safeguards so failed, timeout, disconnected and negative connection
  messages cannot be misclassified as successful recovery events.
- Recent Logs continue to be processed oldest-to-newest, allowing the newest
  recovery event to supersede an earlier disconnect or reconnect failure.
- Increased the recent broker-event deduplication history from 100 to 250 rows.
- Added provider-specific default recovery patterns for TradeStation and Saxo.
- Broker recovery immediately clears CRITICAL state and sends a single recovery
  email only when a critical alert had previously been sent.
- Updated visible and configuration version numbers to 1.03.
- Bridge Protocol V155, AutoTrading reader and Compatibility Framework unchanged.
