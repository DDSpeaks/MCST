MCST-Watchdog 1.06 - Configurable Log Alert Engine

- Adds a dedicated Log Alert Engine for Recent Logs.
- Adds INI-configurable Fatal, Critical, Warning and Ignore keyword lists.
- Sends HTML email alerts for newly observed matching log rows.
- Includes Date/Time, Category, Instrument, Profile, Strategy and Message in alerts.
- Adds duplicate suppression with a configurable time window.
- Does not send historical Recent Logs on startup by default.
- Keeps Log Classification independent from Broker State detection.
- Adds safe INI normalization for all new LogAlerts settings.
- Bridge Protocol V155 and the AutoTrading compatibility profile are unchanged.
