MCST-Watchdog 1.07 - Email Channel Separation and Status Report Settings
=======================================================================

- Separates alert email delivery from report email delivery.
- Alerts use [Email] alert_to, with legacy [Email] to as fallback.
- Status Reports and Heartbeats use [StatusReport] to / [Email] report_to.
- Preserves legacy send_immediately_on_start behavior.
- Honors Status Report weekdays and sending-window settings.
- Adds a Status Report Settings window for recipient, interval, startup delivery, weekdays and sending window.
- Hides AutoTrading research controls unless [Developer] enabled=true.
- Adds explicit queue activity entries identifying the message type and recipient.
- Keeps Heartbeat and Status Report schedules, locks, subjects and recipients independent.
- Bridge Protocol V155 and the verified AutoTrading compatibility profile are unchanged.
