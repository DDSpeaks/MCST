MCST-Watchdog 1.09 - Dashboard Settings Panels

- Added compact three-dot menus to AutoTrading, Status Reports, Email, and Heartbeat dashboard rows.
- Added dedicated AutoTrading, Email, Heartbeat, and Status Report settings windows.
- Added contextual actions for tests, manual reports, heartbeat delivery, report folders, and developer research.
- AutoTrading research actions are available from the row menu only in Developer Mode.
- Removed the redundant bottom Status Settings and Send Test Email buttons from the normal layout.
- Settings are validated, written to MCST-Watchdog.ini, reloaded without startup scheduler semantics, and reflected immediately on the dashboard.
- Alert and report recipient channels remain separate.
- Bridge Protocol V155 and compatibility profiles are unchanged.
